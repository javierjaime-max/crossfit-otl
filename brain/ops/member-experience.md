## The Committed Club

**The mechanic:** attend **15 or more classes in a month** and the **app** puts you in that month's Committed Club. You get **that month's sticker**. Gear follows at milestones — **12 months, 24 months, and beyond**. Twelve months means 15 classes a month, every month, for a year.

**Why it matters — the exact framing (GP):** the Committed Club is the **#1 predictor of two things**:

1. **Who is committed to the community and not likely to be leaving soon.**
2. **Who is likely to achieve personal records and improve their fitness and health.**

**Wording discipline:** it is a **predictor, not a guarantee**. Never promise a result. The honest claim is that people in the club are more likely to keep coming, more likely to learn and adapt, and are the ones whose lives visibly change.

**The social truth, said lightly:** members who stay open to it make real friends here. Some marry each other — **Isabel and Clay met on this floor**. Others end up in each other's weddings.

## Class structure — naming matters

The five parts: **Briefing → Warm-Up → Skill or Strength → The Workout (WOD) → Cool-Down & Recovery.**

- ⚠️ **We do NOT call it "circle time."** The closing block is **intentional cool-down stretching and recovery** — part of the session, not an optional add-on.
- During that block the coach also delivers **announcements** and a **preview of tomorrow's workout**.

**"For time" workouts:** people finish at different times. **Nobody puts equipment away until everyone is finished AND the coach tells the class** they can put it away.

**Starting and finishing on time:** ⚠️ this is **not a "rule"** and must never be written as one. Classes start and finish on time because the next class is waiting — it's **common courtesy**, framed as how we treat the people coming in behind us.

## Naming and voice rules (GP, 2026-08-04)

- **Never write bare "OTL"** in member-facing copy. It is **CrossFit OTL**; the full identity is **CrossFit OTL — On The Line Fitness**.
- **What's on the line? Everything.** Your health, your freedom, your energy, the years you get, whether your kids grow up watching this, your family. Lead with the **O-T-L** letters visually pronounced in *On The Line*.
- **State what things are, not what they aren't.** The gym "was built around life change — because that's what it did for the Jaime family," not "this was never about competitive fitness."
- **The Jaime family = all of them** — Javier & Deanie, Isabel & Clay, Nicholas & Ava.
- Don't announce that **Deanie runs the place** — people figure it out.
- "**Certified CrossFit Trainer**" = **L3 only**. See `people.md`.
