## Class Setup Sequence (Pre-Class)

The coach arrives and must set up screens/apps before the 5AM member walks in. The exact sequence is not yet documented.

**NEEDS_JAVIER — capture the step-by-step:**

1. What app or device controls the workout display? (SugarWOD, Beyond the Whiteboard, custom TV, other?)
2. How does the coach pull up today's workout on the screen?
3. Is there a timer app — what is it, how is it set per workout type (AMRAP countdown vs. for-time clock vs. EMOM)?
4. Any music setup — what system, who controls it, volume standard
5. Any check-in kiosk or iPad for member sign-in — is there one, where, what app

## Score Logging Protocol

From `class-management.md`: the coach must tell members at circle time exactly how to log their score. The logistics of what app is used, and how the coach enters scores or confirms member entry, is not yet documented.

**NEEDS_JAVIER:**

1. What app do members use to log scores? (SugarWOD? Beyond the Whiteboard? Something else?)
2. Does the coach log scores, or do members self-log?
3. Is there a screen that shows the leaderboard during or after the workout?
4. What happens if a member forgets to log — is there a recovery process?

## CAP Programming Display

OTL uses CAP (CrossFit Affiliate Programming by James Hobart). The programming is posted in advance.

**NEEDS_JAVIER:**

1. Where does the coach access CAP programming each week? (App? Website? Printed sheet?)
2. Is there a TV or screen that displays the WOD during class, or is it whiteboard-only?
3. Coaches' notes — what app are they in, and how does the coach access them before the 5AM class?

## Closing / Screen Shutdown

**NEEDS_JAVIER:**

1. What do screens need to be left on or turned off at close?
2. Any devices that need to be plugged in or secured at end of day?
