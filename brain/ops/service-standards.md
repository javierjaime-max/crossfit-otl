# CrossFit OTL — Service Standards

This atom captures what excellent service looks like at OTL — the member experience from the moment they walk in to the moment they leave. Extracted from philosophy and class-management brain atoms; expanded with what's known about OTL's standard.

---

## The OTL Standard

OTL is a family-run affiliate in the Two-Brain Business framework. The experience is not gym-corporate — it is coach-led, community-rooted, and performance-honest. Every service standard flows from that identity.

---

## Before Class

**The coach's job before the first member walks in:**

1. Know the full workout — stimulus, scaling, target time, equipment needed. Not during warm-up. Before anyone arrives.
2. Know the gym setup — who goes where (adults/teens space allocation), where the whiteboard is, where the coach's table sits.
3. Know the safety setup — cones if rig and barbells are near each other, any separation needed between classes.
4. Know the score-logging protocol — exactly what to tell members at circle time about how to log. Do not wing this.
5. Have the coaching notes ready. Best practice (from `class-management.md`): prep all three weeks of posted workouts in one focused hour rather than night-before scrambling.

**Equipment:** Barbells, boxes, and machines should be staged before class — not during warm-up while members stand around.

## During Class

**Known standards (from class-management.md):**

- Communicate the intended stimulus at the start: target time, expected rounds, scaling guidance. Members need to know what they're trying to achieve.
- For "for-time" workouts: nobody puts equipment away until everyone is done. Call this out explicitly at the start.
- For AMRAPs: this isn't usually an issue — but still communicate score-logging at circle time.
- Cap enforcement: coach must manage finish times to keep slow athletes within stimulus and protect class flow for the next wave (especially 5AM → 6AM).
- Barbell sharing protocol: when athletes share, sequence them explicitly (Partner 1 top of minute, Partner 2 at :02, etc.). Don't leave this to members to figure out.
- Cones and safety separations: if the rig and barbells are in close proximity, set cones.

**The 5AM must finish on time, every time.** This is non-negotiable — it sets the rhythm for the entire morning.

## Circle Time and Score Logging

At the end of class, the coach runs circle time. Required elements:

1. Tell members exactly how to log their score — what app, what format, RX vs. scaled designation.
2. Community moment (optional but OTL's culture): shoutouts, PRs, first times.
3. Any upcoming events or announcements.

## The Adults + Teens Together Design

From `philosophy.md`: adults and teens work out simultaneously by design. Parents see their kids across the room. Kids see parents working. This is intentional — not a scheduling workaround.

The service standard implication: coaches running adult and teen classes simultaneously must coordinate on equipment, space, and traffic. This is an above-standard ask — OTL coaches manage a dual-class environment that most affiliates do not.

**Service standard for dual-class days:** Before class, the adult and teen coaches align on: who goes front, who goes back, barbell split, and any equipment conflict. This is not optional — do it before 5AM even if it's via text the night before.

## New Member Experience

**Confirmed standard (GP voice, 2026-07-28):**

- **InBody body composition scan** is standard new-member onboarding — every new member gets scanned.
- **Nutrition walk-through** is included: the coach walks the member through their InBody results — three-component weight breakdown (water, dry lean mass, body fat), body fat targets, how lean mass drives BMR, InBody score out of 100. This is not optional; it is a defined service step.
- **Trainerize white-label app** — ⚠️ **CORRECTED by GP 2026-08-04: this is NOT part of standard new-member onboarding.** The OTL-branded Trainerize app is **only for Nutrition Coaching and Lifestyle Reset clients**. A regular group-class member is never set up on it. (The earlier note here overstated it as a universal onboarding step.)
- **The member-facing booking app is PushPress** (`crossfitotl.pushpress.com`) — that is "the member app" in the welcome packet. Trainerize is a separate coaching app for the two programs above. Do not conflate them.
- InBody scan is also sold as a standalone service ($35, open to non-members).
- The InBody app is downloaded on the member's phone at onboarding; scans sync globally to their account via the same email/phone used at the gym.

## Member Retention Signals

From Javier's coaching philosophy:

- OTL uses InBody data to track body composition change. This is a retention tool — members who see objective progress stay.
- Macros and nutrition coaching are part of the OTL value proposition. The gym is not just "show up and sweat."
- The community (homeschool network, family members across generations, military/law enforcement cohort) is a referral engine — member experience directly drives growth.
