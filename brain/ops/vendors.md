# CrossFit OTL — Vendor Contacts

> This is a skeleton. Every section marked NEEDS_JAVIER requires a voice capture or direct input. Once filled, this becomes the single source of truth for vendor contacts — no more hunting for a number when something breaks.

## How to Add a Vendor

Capture via voice: "vendor contact, [name], [category], [phone/email], [what they handle], [any account number or notes]." Atlas will atomize it here.
