# CrossFit OTL — Safety and Emergency

This atom exists because of a real incident. A member ("Bob") had a **heart attack on the gym floor**. The people around him did the right things — but when they called 911, **nobody could tell the dispatcher the address**. Everything below is written to make sure that never repeats.

---

## The address — the thing that costs the most time

**9155 Blvd 26, Suite 270, North Richland Hills, TX 76180**

- It is **posted on the front door**.
- Every member should be told where it's posted during their first week.
- A dispatcher cannot send anyone until they have an address. This is the single highest-leverage safety fact in the building.

## AED

- **Mounted on the wall right by the water fountains.**
- Point it out to new members on day one — physically walk over to it.
- No training is required to use it. Once opened, it speaks every instruction out loud. **Early use is what saves people.**

## First aid kit

- For everything smaller — torn hands, scrapes, shin cuts — **ask a coach for the first aid kit.**
- Members should not go hunting for it, and should not tough it out.

## If someone goes down — three jobs, assigned out loud

1. **Someone calls 911** and gives the address above.
2. **Someone gets the AED** from the water fountains.
3. **Someone goes outside** to flag the ambulance and walk them in.

The **coach runs the scene**. Anyone not doing one of those three jobs clears the space and keeps the floor open.

## Kids on the floor — flooring rule

- During **any active workout session**, children must remain on the **designated brown wooden floor**.
- Children are **not allowed on the black rubber flooring** during active sessions.
- If a child is on the black floor, they must be **with a parent** and must **not use the equipment**.
- CrossFit OTL is **not childcare**. Parents and guardians are responsible for their child's safety and behavior at all times.

## Barbell drop policy

- **Never drop an empty barbell.**
- **Never drop a barbell loaded only with 10-pound plates.**
- **Change plates** (the small rubber-coated metal plates) are **never dropped**, and should only be on a barbell when accompanied by a **larger-diameter rubber plate**.
- Everything else comes down under control.

## Injuries and history

Members must tell their coach about injuries **before class**, not after the workout — old surgeries, bad shoulders, pregnancy, a rough night of sleep. All of it changes what gets programmed for them that day.

## Recovery and care partners

CrossFit OTL sponsors and partners with local providers in **physical therapy, chiropractic care, and recovery services**. Members should **ask any coach or staff member for a referral** rather than searching on their own.
