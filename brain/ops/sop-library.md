## SOP-002 — Facility Closing

**Who:** Last coach of the day (lead coach of final class)
**When:** After final class ends, before leaving the building

### Steps

1. **All members out.** Confirm no one is remaining on the floor, in the bathrooms, or in any back area. Do a full walk of the building.
2. **Equipment reset.** All barbells back on the rack. Plates sorted and stored. Boxes stacked. Kettlebells in their spot. Rowers and bikes in rest position. Dumbbells on the rack by weight.
3. **Floor sweep.** Sweep or mop if needed — especially if the WOD involved chalk or sand.
4. **Bathrooms.** Check both — paper towels stocked, toilet flushed, no issues. Report any maintenance needs to Javier.
5. **Trash.** Empty trash cans if full. Bags in the dumpster outside. **NEEDS_JAVIER — dumpster location.**
6. **Lights out.** All lights off. Check back area.
7. **HVAC setback.** Set thermostat to nighttime/away setting. **NEEDS_JAVIER — setback temp.**
8. **Lock back door.** Confirm it is locked and the handle does not turn.
9. **Lock front door.** Confirm locked.
10. **Arm alarm.** Code: **NEEDS_JAVIER**. You have [X] seconds to exit after arming. **NEEDS_JAVIER.**
11. **Text confirmation.** Send a text to the OTL coaches group: "Closed [time]. Building clear." **NEEDS_JAVIER — confirm group chat.**

## SOP-003 — Pre-Class Setup

**Who:** Lead coach of each class
**When:** Completed before members arrive

### Steps

1. **Read the workout.** If you have not already done this — stop. Read the programming, identify the stimulus, map the scaling options. This must be done before you touch the equipment.
2. **Identify space assignment.** Adults: front or back? Teens: front or back? Refer to class-management.md for space logic on dual-class days. On dual-class days, coordinate with the other coach before setup begins.
3. **Stage barbells.** Bring barbells to the positions members will use. If barbell count is a constraint (teens + adults sharing), decide the split before class.
4. **Stage plates.** Put the appropriate plates at each barbell starting point (or set up a loading station). Members should not be hunting for a 45lb plate during warm-up.
5. **Set up machines.** Echo bikes and rowers to position. Count how many you need for the rep scheme — if the class is larger than your machine count, program alternating pairs or adjust.
6. **Boxes, kettlebells, dumbbells, jump ropes.** Stage what the workout needs. Position them so traffic flow through the workout does not create collisions.
7. **Cones.** If the rig and barbell area overlap, place cones to separate the zones. This is safety, not optional.
8. **Whiteboard.** Write: Warm-up, Strength (if any), WOD, Stimulus (target time/rounds), Scaling options, Score-logging format.
9. **Confirm AED is accessible and visible.** Equipment staging should never block the AED.
10. **Coaching notes ready.** Your movement cues, scaling standards, and common errors to watch for — have these in your head before the warm-up begins.

## SOP-004 — Equipment Safety Inspection

**Who:** Opening coach (daily) + any coach who notices an issue
**When:** During facility opening, before first class

### Daily Inspection Checklist

**Barbells:**
- [ ] Sleeves spin freely (not seized or grinding)
- [ ] No cracks in the collar area or shaft
- [ ] Knurling present — not worn smooth in grip zone
- [ ] Collars present and locking

**Pull-up Rig:**
- [ ] Wall mounts / floor mounts — no visible movement when tested
- [ ] Bar welds — no cracks
- [ ] Kip swing test — hold a bar and swing moderately; bar should not shift or creak

**Boxes:**
- [ ] No cracked corners or split edges (wooden boxes)
- [ ] No sharp metal edges (steel boxes)
- [ ] Rubber feet intact — box does not slide when loaded

**Echo Bikes:**
- [ ] Seat bolt is tight — no rocking
- [ ] Pedal arms — no wobble
- [ ] Console powers on

**Rowers:**
- [ ] Seat slides smoothly
- [ ] Chain / strap intact — no fraying
- [ ] Console powers on
- [ ] Footboard straps functional

**Kettlebells:**
- [ ] No cracks in the handle
- [ ] No loose coating (cracked rubber/vinyl) that could detach

**Dumbbells:**
- [ ] Heads secure — no obvious wobble on rubber-coated dumbbells

**Jump Ropes:**
- [ ] Handles not cracked
- [ ] Cable intact — no kinks or exposed metal

**Out-of-Service Protocol:**
If you find a defect:
1. Remove the equipment from the floor.
2. Tag it: tape a piece of paper to it that says "OUT OF SERVICE — [date] — [your name] — [brief reason]."
3. Text Javier the same day with a photo.
4. Do not put it back in service until Javier clears it.

## SOP-005 — Member Incident and Injury Reporting

**Who:** Lead coach on duty (any coach who witnesses an event)
**When:** Any injury requiring medical attention, any near-miss, any event that could result in a claim

See the **Emergency Action Plan** for in-the-moment response protocols.

This SOP covers what happens after the immediate emergency is handled.

### Steps

1. **Within 1 hour:** Notify Javier and Deanie if they were not present. Text is fine — include who, what, when, current status.
2. **Within 24 hours:** Complete the Incident Report Form (in the EAP document). Be factual and sequential. Do not speculate about cause. Do not admit fault in writing.
3. **Witnesses:** Collect names and phone numbers of any members who witnessed the event before they leave the gym.
4. **Preserve the scene:** If equipment was involved, do not return it to service until Javier has seen it. If there is any chance of a claim, photograph the equipment and the area before anything is moved.
5. **Submit the form to Javier.** The report goes in the member's file and in the incident binder. **NEEDS_JAVIER — confirm where incident binder lives.**
6. **Insurance notification:** Javier notifies the general liability carrier if the incident is serious enough to result in a medical claim. **NEEDS_JAVIER — carrier name and policy number for reference.**

### What Requires a Report

- Member leaves class to seek medical care (urgent care, ER, doctor)
- Member reports an injury after class that they attribute to the workout
- Significant fall or impact, even if the member says they are fine
- Any head impact
- Any loss of consciousness, even brief
- Equipment failure that caused or could have caused injury
- Any external emergency response (911 called)

Minor scrapes, muscle soreness, and typical training discomfort do not require a formal report. Use judgment — when in doubt, write it down.

## SOP-006 — Class Management, Start to Finish

**Who:** Lead coach
**When:** Every class

This SOP is a sequence. Follow it in order.

### Before Members Arrive

- Equipment staged (SOP-003 complete)
- Whiteboard written
- Coaching notes ready
- Coach is on the floor — not on their phone, not in the back room

### Member Arrival (Class Minus 5 to Start)

- Greet every member by name as they walk in
- If new or unfamiliar face: introduce yourself, brief gym orientation ("We warm up on the floor, whiteboard is there, let me know if you have any questions")
- Coach's job: be available and engaged — not finishing your coffee or checking your phone

### Warm-Up

- Start on time. If the 5AM class starts at 5:00, you call time at 5:00.
- Brief the stimulus at the start of warm-up: "Today is [workout name]. For-time / AMRAP. Target is [X minutes / X rounds]. We're working on [movement]. Here's the scaling ladder."
- Warm-up movements are specific to today's workout — not generic.
- Use the warm-up to identify who needs scaling and address it before the strength or WOD begins.

### Strength (If Programmed)

- Give time caps for each set: "You have 3 minutes to build to your working weight."
- Coach does not disappear to their phone between sets.
- Identify form breakdowns early — address them before the weight goes up.

### WOD Briefing

Before the clock starts, deliver this in order:
1. Workout structure: what movements, in what order, how many reps
2. Stimulus: target time, target rounds, what intensity feels like
3. Scaling: the two most common options, when to use each
4. Score logging: exactly how to record the score (Wodify / paper, format, RX vs. scaled)
5. For-time specific: "Nobody puts equipment away until everyone is done."
6. Barbell sharing if applicable: "Partner 1 goes at the top of the minute, Partner 2 at :02."

### During the WOD

- Coach is moving. You are not standing in one spot watching.
- Call out names for effort, form, encouragement.
- Call corrections loudly enough that adjacent athletes benefit too.
- Monitor the pace — athletes significantly behind the target time get a check-in and a strategy adjustment.
- Cap enforcement: when the time cap hits, call time. Every athlete stops and records their score.

### Circle Time

After the WOD, before members disperse:
1. "Pens down, gather up."
2. Tell them exactly how to log: "Log your score in Wodify. If you were RX, mark it RX. If you scaled, write your modification in the notes."
3. Community moment: PRs, first-time movements, shoutouts, milestones. Keep it real — not every class has one, and that is fine.
4. Announcements: upcoming events, schedule changes, anything Javier has flagged.

### After Class

- Members thank the coach — coach thanks the members.
- Equipment returns to its home (members put away what they used).
- Between-class transition: **NEEDS_JAVIER** — is there a designated between-class cleanup person or does the closing coach sweep?

## SOP-009 — Cleaning and Sanitation

**Who:** Closing coach (deep clean); coaches between classes (quick sweep)
**When:** Between classes (quick) and after final class (full)

### Between-Class Quick Clean (5–10 minutes)

1. Wipe down all barbells with disinfectant wipe or spray + rag — knurling and sleeves.
2. Wipe down pull-up bars.
3. Wipe down any machine (bike handles/seat, rower handle).
4. Reset equipment to starting position for next class.
5. Sweep the floor if chalk or debris is visible.
6. Check bathroom — flush, paper towels.

### End-of-Day Full Clean

1. Mop the main floor. **NEEDS_JAVIER — mop location, cleaning solution.**
2. Wipe all barbells, pull-up bars, machines — full surfaces, not just grip areas.
3. Wipe boxes and kettlebells.
4. Clean mirrors if present. **NEEDS_JAVIER.**
5. Wipe down any countertops, tables, or the coach's desk.
6. Clean bathrooms: sink, toilet, floor.
7. Empty all trash cans.
8. Restock paper towels, soap, and any cleaning supplies that are running low.
9. Notify Javier or Deanie if any supply is out or nearly out.

## SOP-010 — Equipment Failure Protocol

**Who:** Any coach who discovers a failed or unsafe piece of equipment
**When:** During daily inspection (SOP-004) or during class when a failure is discovered

### Steps

1. **Stop use immediately.** If the failure is discovered mid-class, stop the affected athlete(s) from using the equipment. Do this calmly — "Hey, let's swap you to the rower instead."
2. **Remove from floor.** Move the equipment out of the active workout area if possible without disrupting class.
3. **Tag it.** Write "OUT OF SERVICE — [date] — [your name] — [reason]" on paper and tape it to the equipment.
4. **Photograph.** Take a photo of the defect. Send it to Javier with a text.
5. **Substitute the movement.** Inform the class: "[Equipment] is out of service today. Here's your sub: [movement]." Give it without drama.
6. **Do not attempt to repair.** Unless you are specifically authorized to do maintenance on the equipment, do not attempt to fix it. Flag it and let Javier handle it.
7. **Javier clears it.** Equipment returns to service only when Javier confirms it has been repaired or cleared.
