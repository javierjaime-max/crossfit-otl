## Section 2 — Employment Basics

### At-Will Employment

Texas is an at-will employment state. That means either party — you or CrossFit OTL — can end the working relationship at any time, for any lawful reason, with or without notice. This manual does not create a contract of employment.

### Scheduling

Class schedules are set by Javier and Deanie. You are expected to be at the gym and ready to coach at least 15 minutes before your first class. Schedule changes must be communicated to Javier or Deanie at least 48 hours in advance except in genuine emergencies.

If you cannot make a class, you are responsible for finding a qualified substitute and notifying Javier or Deanie that a sub is in place. Do not leave a class uncovered — ever.

## Section 4 — Coaching Standards

### The Non-Negotiables

**Know the workout before anyone walks in.** Every coach reads the programming, identifies the stimulus, maps the scaling options, and notes the equipment needs before the first member arrives. This is not optional — it is the difference between coaching and babysitting.

**Be on the floor before members.** Equipment is staged before class. Barbells are loaded or at least staged. Boxes are in position. Members should not watch you set up the gym on their time.

**Know every name in your class.** OTL is not a globo gym. If you do not know a member's name, learn it by end of class.

**Cap enforcement is not negotiable.** The 5AM class ends on time. Every class ends on time. Members behind pace are capped and cheered on — they do not hold the gym floor past class end.

**The workout is not done until everyone is done.** For for-time workouts, no one puts equipment away while someone is still on the floor. Call this out at the start of class every time.

### Dual-Class Management

When adult and teen classes run simultaneously, the coaches for both classes align before class — not during warm-up. Alignment covers: who takes front, who takes back, barbell allocation, and any equipment conflicts. Do this via text the night before if needed. It must happen.

### Score Logging

At circle time, every coach tells members exactly how to log their score: what app, what format, RX vs. scaled designation. Do not wing this. Do not leave it as "just put it in Wodify" or whatever — give the exact format.

### Scaling

Every member gets a version of the workout that matches their current capacity. Rx is one option. It is not the goal. If a member consistently cannot complete the intended stimulus at Rx, the coach's job is to initiate the scaling conversation — not wait for the member to ask.

## Section 5 — Member Standards and Service

### Greeting

Every member who walks through the door gets acknowledged by name. If you are mid-setup, you look up, make eye contact, and say their name. This is the minimum.

### New Members and Visitors

When a new member or potential member walks in:

1. Stop what you are doing and introduce yourself.
2. Give them a quick layout of the space: "We start on the floor, warm-up runs about 10–15 minutes, here's where the whiteboard is."
3. If they are evaluating membership, let them know Javier or Deanie handles that conversation — and either loop one of them in or take their contact info so Javier can follow up.

Do not let a prospect walk out without a next step.

### Waiver / Liability

**NEEDS_JAVIER:** Confirm where the liability waiver lives (Wodify, paper, both). No new member works out before signing a waiver. This is absolute.

### Complaints

## Section 6 — Safety

### Emergency Action Plan

The CrossFit OTL Emergency Action Plan (EAP) is a separate document posted in the gym. You are required to:

- Read it during onboarding
- Walk through the cardiac arrest protocol on the gym floor before your first solo class
- Participate in the annual drill

The EAP is not theoretical. The Bob Douglas event in July 2026 is why this library exists. Know what to do before you need to know.

### Equipment Inspection

Before every class, the lead coach does a fast sweep of the equipment in use that day:

- Barbell sleeves spin freely, collars lock
- Pull-up bar bolts are secure
- Boxes are not damaged (cracked wood, loose top)
- Echo bike / rower — no obviously loose components
- Kettlebells — handles not cracked

If you find a defect, the equipment is out of service until repaired. Tag it, move it, and tell Javier. Do not let members use broken equipment.

### Incident Reporting

Any injury requiring medical attention — or any incident that could have resulted in one — must be reported to Javier within 24 hours using the Incident Report Form in the EAP. Minor scrapes do not require a form. A member who leaves for urgent care, falls, or reports pain that causes them to stop: report it.

## Section 7 — Conduct Standards

### Conflict of Interest

You may not recruit OTL members for your own training business, independent coaching, or any competing service without Javier's explicit permission. This includes DMing members, giving out your personal contact info for coaching purposes, or promoting your own programs in the gym or on OTL's channels.

### Social Media

If you post about OTL on your personal social media:
- You represent the gym positively.
- You do not share internal business details, member data, or anything that could embarrass the gym.
- Tag @crossfitotl where appropriate — community visibility helps all of us.

You are not authorized to post on OTL's official @crossfitotl account unless Javier has specifically delegated that to you.

### Confidentiality

Member information (names, contact info, health history, billing) is confidential. You do not share it outside the gym. Period.

### Substance Use

You do not arrive to coach under the influence of alcohol or any impairing substance. If a member or other coach shows up impaired, notify Javier immediately.

### Conduct Toward Members

CrossFit OTL has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or intimidation toward any member or staff. If you observe this or experience it, report it to Javier or Deanie immediately.

## Section 8 — Progressive Discipline

We prefer to address issues directly and quickly. If a coaching standard or conduct standard is not being met:

1. Direct conversation — Javier or Deanie discusses the issue with you and what is expected going forward.
2. Written notice — if the issue recurs, you receive a written notice with specifics.
3. Separation — serious or repeated violations result in termination.

Some behaviors result in immediate termination without the above steps: harassment of a member, working impaired, sharing confidential member data, or any act that creates legal or safety liability for the gym.
