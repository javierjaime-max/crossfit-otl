# New Member Welcome Email — ready to paste

Sent at signup. See `asop-new-member-onboarding.md` for where this sits in the sequence.

Voice rules apply: no pricing, no legal language, no routing by name.

---

## Version A — automated (PushPress, on new-member creation)

**Subject:** You're in — start here

> Welcome to CrossFit OTL.
>
> Everything you need for your first ninety days is in one place — how a class runs, who's coaching you, the schedule, and what's actually coming:
>
> **https://crossfit-otl.com/welcome**
>
> Read the "What's coming" page tonight if you read nothing else. It tells you the specific weeks people quit, and why none of them mean it isn't working.
>
> Three things to do this week:
>
> 1. Download the member app and book your first week of classes — deciding in the morning is how the 5AM stops happening.
> 2. Turn on notifications for the Social tab. That's where everything lives.
> 3. Get your free InBody scan if you didn't get one at your consultation. Just ask a coach.
>
> See you on the floor.
>
> — The Jaime Family
> CrossFit OTL · On The Line Fitness

## Notes

- **Link, don't attach.** The page is always current; a PDF in someone's inbox goes stale the moment a coach or a class time changes.
- The PDF exists for the front-desk copy and the in-person hand-off, not for email.
- If this is ever sent before signup, **strip nothing** — the packet already contains no pricing — but know it was written for someone who has already joined.
