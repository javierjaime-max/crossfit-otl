# Join Our Cult(ure)

## Core Campaign Narrative

**Headline:** "CROSSFIT IS A CULT." — WE'VE ALL HEARD IT.

> We're not fighting for aesthetics or quick fixes. We fight disease. Weakness. Decrepitude. Dependence. We're choosing longevity. Community. Strength. We're building lives without limits — the kind you can carry into your 70s, 80s, 90s.
> 
> CrossFit isn't always easy to explain from the outside. But anyone who walks through the door feels it immediately — the support, the effort, the community, the change. If that's a cult, count us in.
> 
> Long live the cult(ure) of health.

With 10,000 gyms in 150+ countries, "cult" was an easy label to weaponize when CrossFit was doing something radically different. Inside affiliates, we're not drinking Kool-Aid — we're fighting actual enemies: disease, weakness, decrepitude, dependence, and a broken system that profits from your sickness.

**Campaign intent:** Not looking for followers. Looking for people tired of "good enough" when it comes to their health. Bold, scroll-stopping statements. Print flyers, post on social, use in paid media, email campaigns asking members to bring a friend.
