# The CrossFit Effect

## Core Campaign Narrative

**Headline:** WE GAVE AWAY THE SECRETS. HERE'S WHAT HAPPENED.

> They said knowledge was something to protect. So we did the opposite. We put our method out there, for free, every single day. Not because we didn't value it. Because we knew where the real value lived. Not in secrecy. In coaching. In standards. In understanding why. So we taught. And then we taught some more. People stopped being followers. They became teachers. Leaders. Community builders. That's The CrossFit Effect.

**Scale proof points:**
- 100,000+ credentialed coaches
- 10,000+ affiliates in 150+ countries
- Seminars running every single weekend globally

**Campaign Tone:** Bold, Inspiring, Authoritative, Human, Purposeful

**Core message for affiliates:**
> Every correction, every cue, every athlete who walks out stronger than they came in is part of something bigger. When people truly understand the *why* behind their training, something shifts. They get healthier. They bring in a friend. That friend brings in their family. The ripple spreads far beyond your four walls and into the communities you live in.

## Three Themes + Content Ideas

### Theme 1: The CrossFit Effect (Methodology)
CrossFit is built on a methodology, a standard, a system of coaching that has been changing lives for 25 years. Help the community see what makes CrossFit different from everything else.

**Content ideas:**
- "What Is The CrossFit Effect?" — post the brand spot with a caption in your own words
- "Because of CrossFit, I…" — ask members and coaches to finish the sentence. Reel, carousel, or graphic series.
- "We Gave Away the Secrets" — challenge community to think differently about what CrossFit really is. Not a workout. A methodology.
- Share Your Spark Moment — coaches share the moment CrossFit changed something for them
- "What Makes CrossFit Different" — why does it work? What separates it from everything else?

### Theme 2: Credentialed Coaches and Education
The person leading class has been taught by some of the best educators in fitness. They know how to move people safely, correct what isn't working, and program with purpose.

**Content ideas:**
- "Meet Your Coach" — beyond name and credential. Who are they outside the gym? What brought them to CrossFit?
- "The Moment Everything Clicked" — the moment CrossFit education changed how they think about coaching
- "Here Is What Your Coach Sees" — pull back the curtain on what a CrossFit trainer is thinking during class
- Mic Your Coaches — movement correction, cue mid-workout, check-in after class. The moments that separate CrossFit coaching.

### Theme 3: The Community
The CrossFit Effect doesn't stop at the gym door. It shows up at home, in families, in neighborhoods.

**Content ideas:**
- "The CrossFit Effect" community stories — how CrossFit changed something beyond fitness. A relationship repaired. A medication dropped. A confidence found. A life rebuilt.
- The Faces of Your Affiliate — diversity of who shows up. Different ages, backgrounds, abilities, goals.
- "Before CrossFit, I…" — what life looked like before. The contrast speaks for itself.
- Tag Someone Who Started Your Chain Reaction — tag the person who introduced them to CrossFit
- Make Your Own CrossFit Effect Video — high-fives, breakthrough moments, sweaty finishes, first pull-ups
