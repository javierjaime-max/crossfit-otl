# The 12-Week Lifestyle Reset — Campaign Brief

## What It Is

The Lifestyle Reset is OTL's flagship transformation program — a fully assembled, 12-week system that combines personal training, group classes, body composition tracking, nutrition coaching, and accountability into one high-commitment, high-result package.

**Tagline:** "THE 12-WEEK LIFESTYLE RESET — Pillars of Total Transformation."

This is not a gym membership. This is a structured intervention for people who are done messing around.

---

## The 5 Pillars

1. **Expert-Led Personal Training** — 3 on-ramp sessions + 9 one-on-one PT sessions (12 total)
2. **Strategic Group Training** — Unlimited classes for 12 weeks (35+ classes per week available)
3. **Body Composition Tracking** — InBody scan every 4 weeks (4 scans total, not guesswork)
4. **Lifestyle & Nutrition Coaching** — 1-hour lifestyle consult + 3 coach check-ins every 4 weeks
5. **Accountability & Committed Club** — Tracking app + attendance tracking + dedicated coach (INCLUDED)

---

## Who It's For

This program self-selects. The right person checks all of these:

- Can you commit to **4× per week** minimum? (Morning and evening classes, daily)
- Can you commit to **12 weeks**? (Real results take a real timeline)
- Are you ready to **invest time and money** in your health? (Both matter)
- Are you ready to be **coachable**? (Trust the process. Follow the system.)

**The more boxes you check, the more this program is built for you.**

OTL does NOT try to convince everyone. The content qualifies people in or out. The wrong person opting out is as valuable as the right person opting in.

---

## The Results

Real, measured outcomes. Not vibes.

Example: **Lanita M.** — 57.4 lbs fat lost, 4.9 lbs muscle gained, -19.6% body fat over 9 months.

The InBody scans every 4 weeks make progress undeniable. Not "I feel better" — actual numbers.

## Voice & Tone for Posts

- **Qualifying, not pitching.** Posts should make the right person lean in and the wrong person opt out.
- **Results-first.** Every post anchors on real transformation — body composition, performance, consistency — not feelings.
- **Direct and confident.** No hedging. No "might help." No "results may vary." OTL coaches know what works and this program is proof.
- **Premium without being elitist.** This is a serious investment for serious people. Not exclusive — demanding. Anyone willing to commit can do this.
- **Urgency through scarcity of commitment, not inventory.** "Not everyone is ready for this" beats "limited spots."
