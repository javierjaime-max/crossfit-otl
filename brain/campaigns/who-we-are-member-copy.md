# Who We Are — member copy

Three pieces. The wall, the email, the app posts. The wall and the email go out the week after the coaches have all seen the clips. The app posts go one a week after that.

Rules carried from the brain: never bare "OTL." Start on time is courtesy, never a rule. Cool-Down & Recovery, not circle time. "Tell a coach," never a name. No guilt.

---

## 2. The email (once)

**Subject:** Who we are

Hey everybody,

Deanie and I have been talking about the things that make this gym what it is. Most of them have never been written down. So here they are.

When somebody here gets their first pull-up, or runs their first mile, or loses fifty pounds, they walk out a different person than the one who walked in. That happens because a coach was right there for the whole hour, and because the people around them stayed in it with them. That's the part we want to protect.

So here's who we are.

**We're the people who wait on the floor until everybody's done.** If you finish early, stay on the floor. You don't have to stand over anybody. Just be there, so nobody finishes to an empty room. And if you finished early, notice it. It usually means there was a little more in you today.

**We're the people who help everybody put their weights away.** When the coach says it's time, put your weights away. Then look for ways to help clear the floor. Somebody else's plates, the clips, the chalk. Keep working until everybody's done, and then we start the cool-down.

**We're the people who show up for the whiteboard.** The Briefing is where your coach tells you what today is supposed to feel like and how to scale it. You can't get that from reading the workout on your phone. Being there on time is how we take care of each other and the class coming in behind us.

**We're the people who give a little more than last time.** Not every day, and not RX. Sometimes it's five more pounds or one more round, when last time felt easy.

One more thing, though, and it matters. None of this is a rulebook. If you have to walk in five minutes late every day for a season because there's no way to get across town, we want you here. If you have to leave early, you're still welcome. Just tell a coach. We'll get you into class, and we'll put your equipment away for you. Being here from minute zero to minute sixty isn't about control. It's about you getting everything you're paying for.

You'll hear the coaches say some of these things out loud for a while. That's on purpose.

See you at the whiteboard.

Javier and Deanie

## 3. App posts (Social tab, one a week, in this order)

### Week 1 — Who we are

Who we are, in four lines.

We're the people who wait on the floor until everybody's done.
We're the people who help everybody put their weights away.
We're the people who show up for the whiteboard.
We're the people who give a little more than last time.

Running late or leaving early? Tell a coach. We'll work with you.

### Week 2 — The floor

Finished a for-time early? Stay on the floor.

You don't have to stand over anybody or get in their ear. Just stay present, so nobody finishes to an empty room. Don't put your weights away until the coach says it's time.

That's the whole reason we do this together instead of in a garage by ourselves. And if you finished early, notice it. That's usually a sign there was a little more in you today. Bring it next time.

### Week 3 — The whiteboard

The hour starts at the whiteboard.

The Briefing is where your coach tells you what today's workout is supposed to feel like, how to scale it, and what to watch out for. You can't get that from reading it on your phone.

So when the coach calls you up, come on up. Shoes on, rope in hand, ready to go. It's how we take care of each other and the class coming in behind us.

### Week 4 — The weights

Everybody helps everybody.

When the coach says it's time, put your weights away. Then look for ways to help clear the floor. Somebody else's plates, the clips, the chalk. Keep working until everybody's done, and then we start the cool-down.

It's not about fair. It's just a group of people that work together.

### Week 5 — More than last time

A little more than last time.

Not every day. Not RX. If last time felt easy, add the two-and-a-half. Add one more round. Ask your coach in the specific warm-up what today's number should be.

That's where the first pull-up comes from. And the first mile. And the fifty pounds.

### Week 6 — What you're paying for

You're paying for a coach, and the coach is going to be right there.

For the whole hour. On the floor, watching, fixing one thing, saying your name. That's what a coached class is, and it's the reason people walk out of here different than they walked in.

If you ever feel like you didn't get that, tell us.
