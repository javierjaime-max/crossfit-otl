# Free Consultation + InBody Scan — Campaign Brief

## What It Is

OTL's primary lead generation campaign. The offer is a free 30-minute consultation that includes a body composition scan on the InBody 270. No workout. No pitch. No commitment. A real conversation about where the person is and where they want to go — plus actual data about their body on the way out.

**The offer in one line:** "Free consultation + InBody 270 body composition scan. 30 minutes. No workout."

This campaign is the top of the funnel. The consultation leads to the Lifestyle Reset (or standard membership) — but none of that is mentioned in this campaign. The post sells one thing: booking the consultation.

---

## The Message — Accountability Through Presence

This campaign targets adults 30–50 who have tried before and stopped. Not beginners. Not people who've never worked out. People who have been disciplined enough to start multiple times and still didn't sustain it.

The reframe: **they didn't fail because they were lazy. They failed because nobody noticed when they stopped showing up.**

The app didn't care. The program didn't check in. Nobody called.

OTL is built differently — coaches track attendance, know your name, and notice when you're gone. That's not a feature. That's the design.

**Thesis line (use as a guide, not verbatim):**
> "None of those things work if you don't show up. And we're the people who notice when you're not here."

---

## Who It's For

- Has started fitness programs before — more than once
- Stopped, not because they were lazy, but because there was no structure pulling them back
- Tired of being the only one responsible for whether they follow through
- Ready to try the thing they haven't tried: a real community with real coaching
- Within driving distance of North Richland Hills — this campaign targets local; a lead who can't come 4x/week is not a lead

Self-selecting signal: someone who will book a free 30-minute conversation is someone willing to invest attention. That's the minimum qualifying bar.

---

## The InBody Angle

The free InBody 270 scan is a tangible deliverable included in the consultation. It's not an add-on or an upsell — it's part of the offer.

Key framing:
- Most people measure progress by feel, by scale weight, by how their clothes fit
- None of those are actual measurements — they're guesses
- The InBody 270 gives: muscle mass, body fat %, water weight, segment-by-segment distribution
- Takes 2 minutes. Gives you a complete picture of where you actually are
- You leave the consultation with real data, not a guess

This angle appeals to the analytical buyer who's been frustrated that they can't tell if what they're doing is working.

## Content Angles

Use these to vary posts across the campaign rotation:

1. **The accountability angle** — Nobody noticed when you stopped. That's the design flaw, not you. *[Core message — use first]*
2. **The scan angle** — "Most people spend years guessing. Come in once and actually know." — InBody 270 as the hook
3. **The environment angle** — You didn't have a willpower problem. You had an environment problem. The right room changes the math.
4. **The start-over reframe** — "You've started before. This time, someone's paying attention."
5. **The data angle** — "We measure what's changing. Every 4 weeks, you see the numbers. Not a feeling. Numbers." *(bridges to Lifestyle Reset tracking without naming the Reset)*
6. **The no-intimidation angle** — "30 minutes. A conversation. No workout. Just come in." — removes the #1 barrier for people who associate CrossFit with being destroyed on day one

---

## Voice & Tone

- **Direct, not aggressive.** This buyer has been oversold. Don't pitch.
- **Name the failure without blame.** "You've started before and it didn't stick" is something they can nod at — not something that shames them.
- **Confident, not aspirational.** No "transform your life." No "your best self." Concrete outcomes, real data, real people.
- **The contrast is felt, never stated.** OTL is never compared to another gym by name. The posts describe what OTL is — the reader draws the contrast themselves.
- Match the voice of the landing page copy (`Fit4/CrossFit OTL/lead-gen/01-landing-page-copy.md`)

---

## What Posts Do NOT Do

- Name the Lifestyle Reset program or price in these posts *(that's the lifestyle-reset campaign's job)*
- Mention HYROX, challenges, or weight-loss transformation angles
- Use before/after framing
- Attack or reference competitors
- Use aspirational fitness clichés ("best version of yourself," "transform your life")
