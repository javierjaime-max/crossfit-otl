# Forging Elite Fitness®

## Core Campaign Narrative

**Headline:** IT WAS NEVER ABOUT ABILITY. IT'S ABOUT WILLINGNESS.

The sequel to CrossFit Is the Cure. A recommitment to CrossFit's core ethos — the bold, the unapologetic, the willing. Forging Elite Fitness® is not new. It's part of CrossFit's DNA, now back at the forefront.

> "It was never about ability, it has always been about willingness — to show up, do the work, and keep progressing. Because we believe human beings are meant to move, grow, refine, and live out the fullest expression of their health."

**Campaign Tone:** Bold, yet approachable. Raw, yet relatable. Intense, yet encouraging.

---

## Three Themes + Content Ideas

### Theme 1: Defining Forging Elite Fitness® (Oct. 20 – Nov. 2)
The true test has never been ability. It's willingness.

**Content ideas:**
- "What does Forging Elite Fitness® mean to you?" — pose to members, coaches, staff
- "This is Elite Fitness" — showcase hard work through video/photo highlighting effort, commitment, progress at any ability or scale
- "Forged In:" — defining moments from the CrossFit experience. "Forged After Work," "Forged with Family," "Forged by Great Coaches"
- Bold Statements — draw from asset library to create graphics, captions, videos

### Theme 2: What Forging Elite Fitness® Looks Like (Nov. 2–16)
"Elite" isn't elitist — it's celebratory. It honors anyone willing to walk through the door, check their ego, listen to their coaches.

Submit short videos (30–60 seconds) tagged @CrossFit to be featured.

**Content ideas:**
- Workouts that push your limits — favorite WOD, benchmark, or movement that represents your fitness journey
- Progress in action — PRs, new skills, the consistency that keeps you improving
- Creative expression — home WODs, outdoor workouts, solo or team efforts
- Your version of elite fitness — lifting heavier, moving faster, scaling smart, showing grit

### Theme 3: Elite Starts With You (Nov. 17–onward)
Through CrossFit, you can forge elite fitness every day — in and out of the gym.

**Content ideas:**
- "My Day 1" — coaches, staff, members reflect on their CrossFit Day 1. Transformations. Why anyone can do CrossFit.
- "My Elite Moment at the Gym" — photo/video of powerful gym moments + statement "My Elite Moment" (top of muscle-up, finishing a WOD, focus before 1RM)
- "My Elite Real-Life Moments" — everyday achievements made possible by CrossFit (running with kids, lifting heavy, moving with confidence)
