# CHAD1000X 2025

## What Is It?

Hero workout honoring **Navy SEAL Senior Chief Chad Wilkinson**, who died by suicide on Oct. 29, 2018 — a result of traumatic brain injuries, blast-wave injuries, and PTSD from 20+ years of deployments. The CrossFit community does this workout annually on **Nov. 11** to raise funds for veteran mental health.

Beneficiary: **The Step Up Foundation** — founded by Sara Wilkinson to change the narrative around veteran suicide, expand awareness of blast-wave injury and TBI. Proceeds support nonprofits focused on veteran brain + mental health.

**Register:** [roguefitness.com/chad1000x](https://www.roguefitness.com/chad1000x)  
**Foundation:** [stepupfoundation.org](https://www.stepupfoundation.org/)

## OTL Angles

- NRH/Mid-Cities has deep military and veteran community — this WOD hits home
- Nov. 11 = Veterans Day — double significance for the community
- Service Cup + CHAD1000X back-to-back in the fall = OTL's veteran appreciation season
- "I'm In" social graphic with member photos = high-engagement content the week before
- Post the Step Up Foundation mission — the *why* behind the workout converts casual participants into donors
- Partner class format = bring a non-member friend, lower barrier to entry for awareness
