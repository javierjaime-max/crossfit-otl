# Share Your Stories — Affiliate Storytelling Initiative

## Core Concept

CrossFit HQ partnered with a PR agency to pitch affiliate stories to **local media outlets** — TV news, newspapers, community magazines, health reporters. HQ pitches; affiliates supply the stories.

Current focus: **Cancer & the CrossFit Community** — how affiliates support individuals and families impacted by cancer.

Submit to: **stories@crossfit.com**

---

## Story Angles HQ Is Looking For

- Cancer survivors who credit CrossFit with helping them recover physically or mentally
- Coaches or gyms supporting a member through diagnosis and recovery
- Affiliates hosting fundraisers, memorial workouts, or charity events for cancer organizations
- Members using CrossFit as part of post-treatment rehabilitation

---

## What Makes a Strong Local Media Story

- A compelling personal journey (challenge, recovery, transformation)
- A strong community component (coaches, members, gym)
- A clear local connection (local resident, event, fundraising)
- A visual element (workouts, community events, group support)

Stories that evoke emotional impact, mark milestones, or highlight community events resonate most.

---

## What to Include When Submitting

- Brief summary (5–7 sentences)
- Timeline of key moments (diagnosis, treatment, recovery milestones)
- Details about the affiliate's role or community support
- Any upcoming events, fundraisers, or milestones
- Photos or videos if available
- Confirmation that the individual is open to speaking with media

**Do NOT send:** General gym promotions, stories without confirmed individuals willing to speak publicly, or unsupported medical claims.

---

## What Affiliates May Gain

- Local media coverage highlighting coaches and members
- Increased community awareness and credibility
- Stronger member connection and retention through shared storytelling
- New member attraction through authentic, mission-driven exposure

---

## OTL Angles

- NRH/Mid-Cities community — local story hook built in
- Any member who has used CrossFit alongside or after cancer treatment
- Charity WODs or memorial workouts we've hosted
- The family gym angle — Isabel coaching at 17, homeschool community roots
- Javier's background — from garage gym to affiliate — is its own comeback narrative
