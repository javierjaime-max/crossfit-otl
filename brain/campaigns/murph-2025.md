# The Murph Challenge 2025

## What Is It?

Murph is a CrossFit Hero WOD honoring U.S. Navy SEAL Lt. Michael P. Murphy (KIA June 28, 2005, Afghanistan). Introduced as a CrossFit Hero workout Aug. 18, 2005. Programmed by thousands of affiliates on Memorial Day every year.

**The Murph Challenge** (since 2014) is the official annual fundraiser for the **Lt. Michael P. Murphy Memorial Scholarship Foundation** — now awards 43+ scholarships annually.
