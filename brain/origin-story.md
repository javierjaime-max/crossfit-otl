# CrossFit OTL — Origin Story

## The Name

OTL = **On The Line**

Originally: the first facility was going to be split right on the border of two cities — literally the border line would run through the middle of the building. "On the Border" sounded like a Mexican restaurant. So: On The Line → OTL.

That building fell through. OTL moved into a different space — still near a city boundary, but not split. The name stayed, and evolved:

> "What's on the line? Your freedom. Your health. Your wellness. Your family. Everything's on the line."

OTL now means **what's at stake in your life** — not geography.

Took about 20 name attempts before OTL was approved by CrossFit HQ as unique enough.

## Before CrossFit — Javier's Story

- Born in Corpus Christi, raised in San Antonio. Fourth-generation Texan.
- Mexican-American. Green eyes. Middle name: Orlando (mom was a Tony Orlando fan).
- High school football player, attended University of Texas at Austin (engineering).
- Met Deanie (wife) senior year of high school — she was in the drum line, he was on the football team.
- Married young. First job: corporate training role, traveling constantly.

**The turning point:** Got off the plane after two weeks on the road. Deanie was waiting at the gate. First thing she said: *"You're fat."*

- Was at ~215 lbs (normally around 180 at 5'10")
- Didn't fight it — put his engineering brain to work
- 6 meals/day, tracked macros meticulously, 5 of 6 meals pre-planned
- Lost the weight, got certified at the YMCA, trained members 5am–7am for three to four years
- Loved coaching from the start — "it was like a therapy session every day"

**Family fitness parallel:** Javier's extended family all have chronic disease — diabetes, medications, struggles. He saw it growing up and decided early: that's not his path. Or his family's.

## The Garage Gym Years (6 years before OTL opened)

1. Javier joins CrossFit (~2011–2013). First gym was terrible: coach showed up late, did the workout behind members. Javier gave it a shot anyway, found a better gym.

2. Becomes obsessed — watched every Sevan documentary, every YouTube video. Wife Deanie never joined his gym.

3. Deanie + her twin sister Lamar + a small group were doing outdoor boot camp in their driveway with a private trainer. Trainer had to quit. Deanie asks Javier: *"Can you write workouts for us?"*

4. Javier starts programming for 4–5 women in the garage. Buys equipment. Puts in rubber floor, pull-up rig, barbells. Three class times emerge:
   - **7am** — the women's class (Deanie's group)
   - **9am** — homeschool football boys (Nicholas and teammates)
   - **4pm** — kids class (Isabel coaching)

5. COVID hits. Kids classes at their regular CrossFit gym don't resume. Kids start coming to the garage. The garage is now running three populations.

6. The team tries to get a classic metal CrossFit building on the border of two cities. Owner/city negotiations drag on a year. City requires $1M in improvements — owner walks. "That almost sank it."

7. Real estate developer friend Daryn Eudaly (father of Nicholas's lifelong best friend Nick Eudaly) helps them navigate. They find a **5,000 sq ft retail space** — 1 mile from home. Not a metal building. A retail space. Bigger than most new gyms are advised to start.

8. **OTL opens in February 2022.** 30 founding members from the garage. One year later: 120 members (adults + teens + kids + personal training).

## Why the Gym Exists — The Real Reason

The gym was built around **Isabel and Nicholas's future**.

Both kids were not going to college. The question: what path instead?

- Isabel (20 at time of podcast): Got her L1 at 17, the same month COVID shut everything down. Had been a CrossFit Kids assistant coach since age 14 at their member gym. Also got Precision Nutrition Level 1 (PN1) at 17. Paid for her own first car by 16 from nannying + coaching wages. Wants to stay home with kids someday — the gym gives her a career path that's flexible and family-compatible.

- Nicholas (17 at time of podcast): Got his L1 at 17. Also ran his own screen printing/apparel business starting around age 14–15 — landed school uniform contracts, learned Shopify, video editing, photography, marketing. Apparel brand: **Made for Glory**.

> "The idea was that they already have more knowledge about fitness and nutrition than most adults who walk through our doors. Why send them to a corporate job when they can bring real value here?"

The gym is their apprenticeship. Deanie runs day-to-day operations as general manager.

## The Homeschool Thread

- Both kids started in private school (Isabel: K–2nd; Nicholas: K). Then Javier and Deanie pulled them for homeschooling.
- Neither parent had been homeschooled. "We thought it was what weirdos do."
- Joined a homeschool co-op that meets once a week with other families + a tutor.
- Kids paid for their own first vehicles through jobs they could do because they weren't in school during the day.
- Isabel's senior thesis: how sugar industry corruption created the food pyramid. Written during COVID.
- The 9am CrossFit class at OTL is de facto the **homeschool PE class** — only homeschool kids are free at that hour. Grows by homeschool referral network.

## The CrossFit Journey That Built It All

- Entire family did Zone, Paleo, Intermittent Fasting, Keto, Carnivore over the years
- Deanie had GERD + skin problems + headaches post-pregnancy. Went Paleo. Symptoms cleared within days. Surgery cancelled. "All we did was eliminate grain and sugar."
- This health transformation is what *committed* the whole family — not competitive CrossFit goals, but life-change goals
- Javier and Isabel took L1 together (February 2019, with Gale Yocum as instructor)
- Javier and Isabel took L2 together right before OTL opened (Feb 2022)
- Javier and Isabel on path to L3 / CCFT together

## Business Infrastructure

- **Programming:** CrossFit Affiliate Programming (CAP) — James Hobart. OTL loves it for beginner/intermediate clarity and the L1-refresher-every-day structure.
- **Business mentor:** Two-Brain Business (Chris Cooper). Mentor: Karen Hazelton (growth phase). Previously Jeff Puka (on-ramp/launch phase).
- **Sales process:** Two-Brain no-sweat intro framework.
- **Member management:** Reservation system with photos on file so every coach knows every member by face before they walk in.
- **Rule:** When someone walks in, say hi and use their name. Always.
- **Corporate structure:** Fit4 Associates Inc. (S Corp) — holding company. CrossFit OTL operates as a DBA.
